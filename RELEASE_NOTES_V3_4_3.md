# v3.4.3 — Index Assignment & Legacy Backfill
- Unassigned Assets are now visibly flagged in Asset Registry.
- Bulk-select legacy Assets and classify them as Flagship, Supporting, or Niche.
- Plekxa automatically chooses/creates the correct 20-Asset Index; staff never manually chooses an Index.
- Fixed economics: 4 Flagship @ 8%, 6 Supporting @ 6%, 10 Niche @ 3.2% = 100%.
- Asset editor includes Index classification and locks it after assignment.
- Saving a classified unassigned Asset automatically assigns it.
- Accepted commissioned deliverables continue to auto-assign on acceptance.
- Certificate generation is idempotent to prevent duplicate certificates.
