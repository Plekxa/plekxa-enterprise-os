# Plekxa Enterprise OS v3.6.0
- One Project reserves exactly one primary Asset and Index at creation.
- Index assignment is diversified by genre/mood; 20 equal 5% Asset positions.
- New Project Production Workspace for commissioning a Director and contributors.
- Director and contributor ownership allocations attach to the reserved Asset and may remain provisional until finalisation.
- Private Commission workspaces remain separate per creator.
- Applicants continue to submit fit/portfolio applications and cannot access instrumentals/reference files before acceptance.
- Asset finalisation requires contributor ownership to total exactly 100%; certificates are issued at finalisation rather than on the first accepted deliverable.
- Collections remain independent many-Asset groupings and can compile completed/released Assets into albums, EPs or playlists.
