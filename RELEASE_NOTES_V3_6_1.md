# Plekxa Enterprise OS v3.6.1 — Project Hub consolidation

- Projects are now the operational hub for applications, commissions, milestones, deliverables, Director assignment and Asset contributors.
- Removed standalone Applications, Deliverable Review, External Commission and Commissions entries from Enterprise navigation. Existing routes remain for backward compatibility.
- Renamed Proposals to Creator pitches and clarified that a pitch exists before a Project; an approved pitch creates a Project.
- Project type, genre and mood are dropdown taxonomies backed by Supabase. Staff can add options inline.
- Removed Total budget from Project create/edit. Creator/commission pay and pay details remain.
- Milestones are schedule/checkpoints; deliverables are concrete outputs assigned to a private creator commission.
- Directors can be directly appointed from the Creator Directory; they do not have to apply. Accepted applicants may also be appointed.
- One Project still produces one primary Asset; Collections can compile Assets from multiple Projects.
